# Privacy Policy
**Coroner Report**

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Developer:** Code 3 Coders LLC  
**Website:** https://github.com/savicious  
**Contact:** support@code3coders.com

## 1. Introduction

Code 3 Coders LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information entered into Coroner Report.

Coroner Report is an iOS application designed to assist law enforcement personnel with preparing the Contra Costa County Coroner Initial Telephone Summary.

The application is designed to function locally on your device without requiring an account, cloud connection, or transmission of case information to Code 3 Coders LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Coroner Report.

## 2. Information Collection

**Code 3 Coders LLC does not collect, receive, or store personal or investigative information entered into Coroner Report on its servers.**

The application does not require account registration or personal information to access its core functionality.

Information entered by users may include:

- Decedent identifying and contact information
- Driver's license information
- Incident and investigative details
- Medical history and medication information
- Reporting officer and agency information
- Other information necessary to complete the Coroner Initial Telephone Summary

This information is processed and stored locally on the user's device.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Coroner Report stores case information locally on your device to support report preparation, saved cases, and PDF generation.

Code 3 Coders LLC does not maintain a remote database of case records and does not automatically upload report contents to company-operated cloud servers.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security and managing locally stored case information in accordance with their agency's policies.

Information stored locally may be included in device backups depending on the user's iOS and backup settings.

## 4. Driver's License Scanning

Coroner Report offers optional driver's license scanning to assist with entering identifying information.

Information obtained through this feature is used to populate applicable report fields.

Scanned information is not transmitted to Code 3 Coders LLC.

Users should review all automatically populated information for accuracy before generating or submitting a report.

## 5. PDF Generation and Information Sharing

Coroner Report allows users to generate a PDF version of the completed Coroner Initial Telephone Summary.

Users may choose to:

- View the completed report on their device
- Share the PDF through email
- Send the PDF through text messaging
- Print the report using AirPrint
- Save the PDF to their device
- Share the report using other supported iOS sharing options

**Information is shared only when the user initiates a sharing or export action.**

Once information is shared through another application or service, its handling is subject to that service's privacy and security practices.

Code 3 Coders LLC does not control how third-party messaging, email, printing, or file-storage services handle information shared by users.

## 6. Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

Coroner Report does not use report contents for advertising, marketing, profiling, or artificial intelligence processing.

The application does not:

- Sell personal or investigative information
- Display third-party advertisements
- Upload case information to Code 3 Coders LLC servers
- Use third-party analytics to collect report contents
- Share case information with data brokers

Coroner Report uses native Apple iOS functionality for supported features such as PDF viewing, document sharing, printing, and scanning.

Apple and other services selected by users may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 7. Data Security

Coroner Report is designed to keep case information on the user's device rather than transmitting it to developer-operated servers.

Users should protect their devices using appropriate security measures, including device passcodes, biometric authentication where available, and current iOS security updates.

Because the application may contain sensitive law enforcement and medical information, users must follow applicable agency security requirements, including policies governing confidential investigative records.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Case information remains locally stored on the user's device until removed through available application or device-management functions.

Users are responsible for managing saved reports and exported documents in accordance with their agency's records-retention requirements.

Removing information from Coroner Report does not necessarily delete copies previously exported, printed, emailed, backed up, or saved through other applications.

Code 3 Coders LLC cannot remotely access, retrieve, or delete locally stored case information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Coroner Report is intended for professional law enforcement use and is not designed for children.

Code 3 Coders LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Information about minors may be entered as part of legitimate law enforcement reporting activities and remains subject to the local storage and user-directed sharing practices described in this policy.

## 10. Law Enforcement and Agency Responsibilities

Coroner Report is an independent reporting assistance tool.

It is not an official application of Contra Costa County, the Contra Costa County Sheriff's Office, or the Coroner's Office.

Users and their agencies are responsible for:

- Verifying the accuracy of information entered
- Following applicable reporting procedures
- Protecting confidential investigative information
- Complying with applicable laws and agency policies
- Using approved methods to transmit sensitive records
- Meeting applicable records-retention requirements

The application does not replace official agency reporting systems or procedures.

Code 3 Coders LLC may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Updates will be published with a revised effective date.

The current version of this Privacy Policy will be made available through the developer's GitHub page.
