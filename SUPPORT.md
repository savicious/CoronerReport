# Coroner Report Support

Welcome to the official support repository for **Coroner Report** by **Code 3 Coders LLC**.

Coroner Report is a mobile law enforcement documentation application designed to streamline the preparation of the **Contra Costa County Coroner Initial Telephone Summary** through guided data entry, locally saved case records, and PDF generation.

The application allows officers to complete, review, and share coroner summaries directly from their iPhone without relying on traditional paper forms.

---

# Support Requests

For bug reports, feature requests, troubleshooting assistance, or general support, please contact the development team directly through GitHub.

## How to Request Support

### 1. Open a GitHub Issue

Please create a new Issue in this repository and include:

- Device model
- iOS version

- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Screenshots, if applicable

Providing detailed information helps the development team diagnose and resolve issues more efficiently.

**Important:** Do not include personally identifiable information, criminal justice information (CJI), actual decedent information, medical records, completed coroner summaries, or confidential investigative details in support requests.

# Current Application Scope

Current releases of Coroner Report focus on:

- Contra Costa County Coroner Initial Telephone Summary generation
- Guided, step-by-step case documentation
- Decedent identification and demographic information
- Optional driver's license scanning and automatic field population
- Incident and death investigation information
- Medical history and medication documentation
- Reporting officer and agency information
- Locally saved case summaries and incomplete drafts
- PDF generation and preview
- Sharing completed summaries through text messaging and email
- AirPrint support
- Saving completed PDFs directly to the device
- Reading completed summaries directly from the application during telephone notifications
- Offline access to core reporting functionality

Coroner Report currently supports the **Contra Costa County, California**, reporting format.

Future updates may expand county coverage and introduce additional documentation features based on user feedback.

# Privacy and Data Handling

Coroner Report is designed with a local-first storage philosophy.

- No cloud storage of case information operated by Code 3 Coders LLC
- No external AI processing of user-entered investigative information
- No third-party sale of user data
- No user account required
- Core reporting functionality operates locally on the device
- Saved cases remain stored locally unless the user intentionally exports or shares information
- PDF reports are generated from information provided by the user
- Optional driver's license scanning is used to assist with data entry

Information intentionally shared through email, messaging, printing, or other iOS services may be subject to those services' privacy and security practices. Locally stored information may also be included in device backups depending on device settings.

Users should follow their agency's policies concerning the storage, handling, transmission, and retention of sensitive investigative and medical information.

Please review the official **Coroner Report Privacy Policy** for additional information.

# Important Notice

Coroner Report is a law enforcement documentation assistance tool. It does not replace:

- Officer judgment and discretion
- Agency policies and procedures
- Official coroner reporting requirements
- Required telephone notifications
- Death investigation protocols
- Applicable California laws and regulations
- Medical examiner or coroner determinations
- Supervisory review or investigative oversight

Users remain responsible for independently verifying all information entered into the application and ensuring completed reports comply with applicable agency and coroner requirements.

Coroner Report does not independently verify investigative findings, determine cause or manner of death, or submit reports automatically to the Coroner's Office.

**Coroner Report is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official application of Contra Costa County, the Contra Costa County Sheriff's Office, the Coroner's Office, or any other government or law enforcement agency.**
